About Our Academy

More Than Football

Black Stars Soccer Academy is based in Kiboga District, Uganda. Our mission is to provide young players with a positive environment to learn football, develop discipline and teamwork, and grow into confident young people.

We welcome boys and girls aged 5–18 years.

The academy was started in 2018 with the aim of giving young people in our community an opportunity to develop through football.

Support Young Talent

Black Stars Soccer Academy welcomes individuals, companies, organisations and partners who want to support young footballers and help create opportunities for children in Kiboga.

Sponsorship can support football development, education, girls' football, equipment, transportation, competitions and player development.

⚽ Football Sponsorship

Sponsors can support young players through football-related needs such as training equipment, football kits, transport, competitions and player development activities.

Possible areas of support

  • Football equipment
  • Training kits
  • Competition transportation
  • Tournament participation
  • Coaching and training activities
  • Girls' football development

📚 Education Sponsorship

❤️ Choosing a Child to Support

A sponsor may choose to support a specific child or allow the academy to identify a child who needs support.

Selection considerations

  • Financial need
  • Commitment to football
  • Training attendance
  • Age and education
  • Family circumstances
  • Long-term development needs
