About Our Academy

More Than Football

Black Stars Soccer Academy is based in Kiboga District, Uganda. Our mission is to provide young players with a positive environment to learn football, develop discipline and teamwork, and grow into confident young people.

We welcome boys and girls aged 5–18 years.

Black Stars Sponsorship

Your support can help young athletes develop their football talent, continue their education and prepare for a successful future.

Black Stars Soccer Academy welcomes individuals, families, companies, organizations and partners who would like to support the development of young people through football and education.

⚽ Football Sponsorship

Support the development of young footballers at Black Stars Soccer Academy through training, competitions, equipment and player development.

Your sponsorship can support:

  • Football and training equipment
  • Playing and training kits
  • Transport to matches and tournaments
  • Competition fees
  • Coaching development
  • Girls' football
  • Player development
  • Training facilities

🎓 Black Stars Education Sponsorship

Black Stars Education Sponsorship facilitates our scholarship program, which aims to give our athletes an education that gives them a chance to be successful in a difficult and changing world.

Our scholarship is planned to run for the entire period that the athlete remains with Black Stars Soccer Academy, subject to the sponsorship arrangement.

Monthly Education Sponsorship

$35

Support the educational needs of a young athlete each month.

Yearly Education Sponsorship

$1,000

Provide broader annual support for education and development.

📅 Length of Sponsorship

Sponsors can choose the period they would like to support an athlete or academy program.

  • 1 month
  • 6 months
  • 1 year
  • Multi-year sponsorship
  • Ongoing sponsorship

For education sponsorship, longer-term support can provide greater stability for the athlete's education and development.

👥 Group Sponsorship

A company, organization, family or group of individuals can sponsor several athletes together.

Examples:

  • 5 athletes × $35/month = $175/month
  • 10 athletes × $35/month = $350/month
  • An entire age group
  • The girls' football program
  • A football team
  • A specific academy project

The academy can work with the sponsor to agree on an appropriate sponsorship arrangement.

🎁 What Do You Get With Your Sponsorship?

Sponsors can receive appropriate updates about the impact of their support, depending on the sponsorship arrangement.

  • 📸 Updates about the sponsored program or athlete
  • 📊 Progress reports
  • ⚽ Football development updates
  • 🎓 Education progress updates
  • 🏆 Competition and achievement updates
  • 🤝 Recognition as an academy supporter
  • 📄 Sponsorship acknowledgement or certificate where applicable
  • 📢 Sponsor recognition on academy communication platforms where agreed

Sponsorship does not guarantee selection for a team, professional contract, international opportunity or future employment.
